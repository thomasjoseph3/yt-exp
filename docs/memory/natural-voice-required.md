The owner rejected the first voice: "too AI generated... too slow and pausing... no one will stick to this audio". Narration must feel real and natural.

**Why:** Retention and the channel's income depend on it, and robotic AI voice is also a risk under YouTube's inauthentic-content policy.
Update 2026-10-27: at ~175 wpm the owner said "the audio is too fast now, we need to slow a little bit"; target is now 150 wpm (relaxed but lively), takes outside ±7% are gently adjusted.
**How to apply:** Keep the one-take generation (no per-scene clips or gaps), the ~150 wpm target, conversational scripts written for the ear, and vivid director-style voice direction. Before claiming a voice change is good, produce a real sample and let the owner hear it (Telegram). Related: [[thoma-channel-project]].
